Core mechanic

You only get one

ONE BULLET is built around a single recallable shot. Once the bullet leaves your chamber, you are completely vulnerable. The only way back to safety is movement, timing, and control.

01

Fire

Commit to the angle. The shot leaves the gun and changes the entire fight instantly.

02

Vulnerable

No backup round. No easy recovery. You survive through movement while pressure closes in.

03

Recall

Call the bullet back through the crowd and turn the return path into a weapon.

04

Chain

Great runs come from rhythm. Fire, evade, recall, reposition, then do it again cleanly.

Gameplay features

Sharp and punishing

Clean movement, ruthless pressure, and one round that turns positioning into the whole game.

⚡

Dash-first combat

Fast movement and tight arenas. You survive by reading pressure and choosing angles quickly.

◎

Recallable piercing bullet

One round, two threats. Outgoing fire and return path both matter.

☢

Brutal enemies and bosses

Mutated squads, shielded threats, and bosses that punish hesitation immediately.

Enemies & bosses

Everything wants your angle

Different enemy roles exist to break your timing, block your lines, and punish the moment after you shoot.

C

Chargers

Close distance fast and force movement checks the moment your chamber is empty.

S

Shooters

Turn safe lanes into death and make simple recalls much harder to line up.

B

Shielded

Block direct shots and demand smarter return angles or cleaner positioning.

X

Splitters

Multiply pressure if ignored and flood the arena when timing falls apart.

Bosses should feel grotesque and unfair.

Toxic zones, collapsing space, projectile storms, and brief recall windows. Your only real advantage is perfect spacing and perfect timing.

World & story

Chernobyl after the break

Post-apocalyptic Chernobyl

Twisted physics

Last survivor

Your squad is gone. Your gear is gone. All you have left is one weapon and one round.
