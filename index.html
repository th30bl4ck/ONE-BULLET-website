ONE BULLET

You've got one bullet. Fire it, and you're defenseless until you yank it back. Keep moving, line up your shots, and use the bullet's return path to tear through crowds.

01 Bullet
∞ Ways to die
Core Mechanic

YOU ONLY GET ONE

01

FIRE

Shoot to kill. The moment the bullet leaves the barrel, you are left vunerable.

02

VULNERABLE

You don't have backup ammo. When you're empty, you need to recall your bullet.

03

RECALL

Pull the bullet back to you. It rips through anything stupid enough to stand in its way.

04

CHAIN

Get into the rhythm. Shoot, dodge, recall, reposition. Don't break the loop.

Gameplay

FAST &
UNFORGIVING

It’s all about where you stand and when you move. so keep moving.

DASH-FIRST COMBAT

Dash around and dodge the enemies trying to attack you or shoot at you.

PIERCING RECALL

You have two chances to hit someone once when you pull the trigger, and again when you call it back.

BRUTAL ARENAS

Massive bosses and mutated swarms. That you have to use your enviroment to get away from.

The Threat

THEY WANT YOU DEAD

C

CHARGERS

They rush you, punishing predictable movement.

S

SHOOTERS

They shoot bullets make you have to constantly have to keep moving.

B

SHIELDED

You can't hit them head on. You'll have to get creative with your recall angles.

X

SPLITTERS

Kill them and they will duplicate into smaller enemies.

A BROKEN WASTELAND

Explore the cracked concrete and flooded ruins of a zone where the rules of physics stopped applying a long time ago. Fight through the rusted remains of a dead power plant. Your team is gone.

Replayability
STORY RUNS Fight your way to the center of the zone and figure out what went wrong.
ENDLESS The enemies keep spawning until you finally make a mistake.
BOSS RUSH Skip the grunts and fight every monstrous boss back-to-back.
DAILY SEEDS Everyone gets the exact same layout for 24 hours. Compete for the top spot.
